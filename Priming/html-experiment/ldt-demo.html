Lexical Decision Task

How it works

On each trial you will see:

Fixation cross → Prime word → Target string

Your task is to decide as quickly and accurately as possible whether the target is a real English word or a nonword.

You will start with 12 practice trials to get familiar with the task, followed by 80 experimental trials. You have 5 seconds to respond on each trial.
