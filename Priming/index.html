Module overview

The goal of this module is for you to learn what the priming paradigm is, get an idea of some of the research questions it can be used to study, and familiarize yourself with different kinds of priming experiments. This will involve a mixture of hands-on work and literature searching. By the end of the module, hopefully you will be able to do the following things:

  • Explain the design of a priming experiment and the logic behind it;
  • Explain what the priming effect is and think about the results of a sample experiment done by our class;
  • Examine a published psycholinguistic study in order to understand what it is about and to find specific information;
  • Identify different ways priming experiments can be carried out, in terms of (1) the prime-target relationship; (2) the presentation paradigm; and (3) the participant's task.

Module instructions

This module includes five tasks. For each task, you will need to read something and then answer some questions; some of these questions may require a long time to think about. You should do the tasks in the order they are presented here, because each task builds off of the previous one. You will need to complete tasks 1-4 before class in week 3.

Next to each task I have written an estimate of how much time you might need to complete the task. This is, of course, a rough estimate, and the real time may be different for different students.
